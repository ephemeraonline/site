welcome message

hi! welcome to our page. we are ephemera - a small creative studio run by two friends with too many browser tabs open. we carved out this corner of the internet as a place to experiment freely and let ideas exist without needing to be perfect or permanent.

we built this space as a love letter to the simpler times of the early internet: personal pages, messy layouts, and the joy of clicking links just to see where they go. back when the web felt smaller, weirder, and not that serious.

our site is a digital scrapbook - you’ll find bits of design, past projects, stuff we like and more. some features may stick around and some might vanish. we’re still figuring it out :)

so click around, get lost, and have fun. thank you for being here <3
